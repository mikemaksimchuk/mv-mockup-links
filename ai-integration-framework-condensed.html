Stage 0 · New

Surfacing

See what is already happening, without blame.

MOVE ON WHEN: someone in authority has named the reality out loud and owns the next decision.

NIRN Stage 1

Exploration

Decide whether — and what — before deciding how.

MOVE ON WHEN: a written go / no-go / not-yet decision, a named team with authority, committed time and budget.

NIRN Stage 2 · New

Installation

Build everything the first day of use will depend on.

MOVE ON WHEN: policy adopted, contracts signed, pilot staff trained, supports scheduled. Miss one and you are still installing.

NIRN Stage 3

Initial Implementation

First real use. Expect the dip; support through it.

MOVE ON WHEN: evidence it works in your context, refined procedures, a scaling plan with named supports.

NIRN Stage 4

Full Implementation

Ordinary, monitored, sustained — and scheduled for renewal.

RENEWAL: a new statute, federal action, capability shift, or 12 months reopens the affected chutes.

Horizon · from 2023

Innovation

Aspirational. Nobody has figured this out yet.

NO GATE. Not a stage and not a reward for finishing. Several domains have no innovation state, and that is the honest answer.
